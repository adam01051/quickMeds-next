# QuickMeds Premium Healthcare UI Skill

Use this skill when improving QuickMeds pharmacy platform pages, especially homepage, product listing, medicine detail pages, prescription upload, cart, checkout, and account pages.

## Design Goal

QuickMeds should feel:
- premium
- clinical
- calm
- trustworthy
- modern
- fast
- easy to use
- pharmacy/healthcare-specific

Avoid styles that feel too playful, futuristic, crypto-like, gaming-like, or overly glassy.

## Visual Direction

Use a "premium clinical commerce" design style.

The UI should combine:
- clean medical whitespace
- soft green/blue healthcare accents
- high readability
- premium shadows
- calm rounded cards
- clear CTAs
- trust badges
- professional product cards
- restrained animation

## Recommended Color System

Primary:
- Deep medical green: #0F766E
- Premium teal: #14B8A6
- Clinical blue: #2563EB

Background:
- Main background: #F8FAFC
- Soft medical background: #EEFDF8
- Card background: #FFFFFF

Text:
- Primary text: #0F172A
- Secondary text: #475569
- Muted text: #94A3B8

Borders:
- Soft border: #E2E8F0
- Premium border: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)

Status:
- Success: #16A34A
- Warning: #F59E0B
- Danger: #DC2626
- Info: #0284C7

Do not use too many bright gradients. Use gradients only for hero background, CTA accents, or small badges.

## Typography

Use a clean premium font stack:
- Inter
- Manrope
- Plus Jakarta Sans

Recommended:
- Headings: font-semibold or font-bold
- Body: font-normal
- Buttons: font-medium
- Avoid overly thin text

Suggested sizes:
- Hero title: text-4xl to text-6xl
- Section title: text-2xl to text-4xl
- Card title: text-base to text-lg
- Body: text-sm to text-base
- Small label: text-xs

## Layout Rules

Use generous spacing:
- Section padding: py-16 to py-24
- Card padding: p-5 to p-8
- Navbar height: h-16 or h-20
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8

Use consistent radius:
- Buttons: rounded-full or rounded-xl
- Product cards: rounded-2xl
- Hero cards: rounded-3xl
- Inputs: rounded-xl or rounded-full

Use shadows carefully:
- Basic card: shadow-sm
- Premium hover: shadow-lg
- Floating elements: shadow-xl
- Avoid harsh black shadows

## Healthcare Trust Elements

Add trust elements near CTAs:
- Licensed pharmacy badge
- Secure checkout badge
- Fast delivery badge
- Pharmacist support badge
- Prescription verified badge
- Genuine medicine badge

Example labels:
- "Licensed Pharmacy"
- "Secure Prescription Upload"
- "Verified Medicines"
- "Pharmacist Support"
- "Fast Delivery"
- "Private & Secure"

## Homepage Sections

Recommended homepage order:

1. Premium navbar with search
2. Hero section with medicine search
3. Trust badges row
4. Popular categories
5. Prescription upload CTA
6. Featured medicines/products
7. How QuickMeds works
8. Pharmacist support / consultation section
9. Health essentials / wellness products
10. App/download or delivery section
11. Footer with license/contact/trust info

## Product Card Rules

Product cards should feel premium and trustworthy.

Each product card should include:
- clean product image area
- category or tag
- product name
- short subtitle or dosage if available
- price
- stock status
- "Add to cart" button
- optional prescription-required badge

Avoid clutter. Do not show too much text.

Example badges:
- "Prescription Required"
- "OTC"
- "In Stock"
- "Fast Delivery"
- "Best Seller"

## Button System

Primary CTA:
- bg-teal-700 or bg-emerald-700
- white text
- rounded-full
- hover slightly darker
- subtle shadow

Secondary CTA:
- white background
- soft border
- dark text
- hover bg-slate-50

Danger CTA:
- red only for destructive actions

Avoid neon colors.

## Accessibility

Healthcare UI must be readable and accessible:
- high contrast text
- clear focus states
- large tap targets
- no tiny buttons
- no important text over complex backgrounds
- avoid too much transparency
- form fields need labels or aria-labels
- errors must be clear and close to inputs

## Avoid

Do not use:
- heavy glassmorphism everywhere
- too many gradients
- excessive animation
- tiny fonts
- low contrast placeholder text
- overloaded navbars
- crypto/AI-style glowing cards
- playful cartoon pharmacy design
- generic template look
